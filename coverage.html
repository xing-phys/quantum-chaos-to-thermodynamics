This map uses the numbered topic inventory of D’Alessio et al.  [1]. Each row identifies where to learn the corresponding subject here. The book supplies independent explanations and examples; the original review remains the source for its particular historical discussion and numerical datasets. All seven appendices are included in the topic map.
